One Parlay of the Day per game day: 3 PRA legs from the greenest stars on the board (book PRA line 30.5+, our projection furthest over it), one per game, each -300 or longer, about +100 to +300 together. If nothing fits, legs up to -350 are allowed, then 2 legs. A first pick is made the evening before from the books' real lines and refreshed by each run until the morning odds run, which re-picks it from the full morning board (players whose lines post overnight included) and locks it. Later runs re-check it and flag injuries. Last night's stays up until it's graded the next morning.
Model output, not betting advice. For entertainment among friends. If gambling stops being fun: 1-800-GAMBLER. A parlay needs every leg to hit — high conviction per leg still compounds into real risk across three or four of them.
