What this is (planned)

A hobby model projecting NBA player points, compared against sportsbook over/under lines, ranking any positive-expected-value edges — the same approach as the K Board's MLB strikeout model, adapted for basketball. This page, like the rest of this site, is currently a design mockup: the layout and interactions are real, the data is not.

Today board

Table-only by design, not cards: a full NBA slate runs 20+ meaningfully-projected players a night (vs. the K Board's ~15 probable starters), so a scannable sortable table beats scrolling through cards. One row per player, one column each for points, rebounds and assists — tap any of those headers to sort the whole slate by that stat's projection, so "who's projected for the most points/rebounds/assists tonight" is a single tap away.

Edge coloring

Each projected number is colored by how far it sits from that stat's book line, as a percentage of the line: green when the projection clears the line by 6% or more (model likes the over), red when it's 6% or more under the line (model likes the under), and gold in between, where the model roughly agrees with the market. A relative (percentage) threshold, rather than a fixed point gap, is what lets one rule work sensibly across points, rebounds and assists despite their very different typical ranges. The small number under each projection is that stat's current book line.

Jokic TD tracker

A separate tab tracking Nikola Jokic's triple-double prop specifically — his favorite bet and a fun one to track on its own, given how often it hits. The season summary and recent-games log there are his real 2025-26 numbers; the "Tonight" projection is a placeholder until a real triple-double model exists (a proper one needs a joint probability across points/rebounds/assists, not three independent point estimates multiplied together — see the planning doc for more on why that's harder than it sounds).
